Our Mission

Bridging Africa's digital divide — one young coder at a time.

Millions of talented young people across Africa are blocked from tech careers not by a lack of ability, but by a lack of access. TEYA changes that. We provide computers, structured coding education, and world-class mentorship to high-performing students from low-income backgrounds in Nigeria and Kenya.

Our graduates will become the engineers, data scientists, and innovators who build the next generation of African technology — and drive economic growth across the continent.

Roadmap

Six phases.
One transformation.

01

Planning & Research

Defining beneficiaries, building our organisational structure, establishing non-profit legal status in Nigeria, Kenya, the USA, and Europe, and laying the groundwork for key partnerships.

In Progress
02

Partnerships & Resources

Securing donated or refurbished computers from tech companies, partnering with coding platforms for free or subsidised courses, and establishing physical learning centres in Lagos and Nairobi.

Upcoming
03

Fundraising & Budgeting

Crowdfunding campaigns, grant applications to major foundations, corporate donations, and fundraising events featuring voices from the global tech industry.

Upcoming
04

Programme Implementation

Launching coding classes and an online learning portal, running a structured mentorship programme, and building a collaborative community across specialisation tracks.

Upcoming
05

Monitoring & Evaluation

Tracking enrolment, completion, and job placement rates — targeting 70% placement within six months. Publishing transparent annual impact reports for all stakeholders.

Upcoming
06

Expansion & Sustainability

Scaling to Ghana, South Africa, and Ethiopia. Adding new career tracks and building sustainable income through professional bootcamps and a corporate sponsorship model.
