Our Mission

Bridging Africa's digital divide — one young coder at a time.

Millions of talented young people across Africa are blocked from tech careers not by a lack of ability, but by a lack of access. TEYA changes that. We provide computers, structured coding education, and world-class mentorship to high-performing students from low-income backgrounds in Nigeria and Kenya.

Our graduates will become the engineers, data scientists, and innovators who build the next generation of African technology and drive economic growth across the continent.

Who We're Targeting

Young people aged 13–24 in Kenya with little or no practical computer experience. Many are bright, motivated students who have simply never had consistent access to a device. Our pilot cohort will recruit from students graduating high school in December 2026.

Pilot Launch
🖥️

Starting From Zero

We don't assume any prior knowledge. TEYA Kenya begins with introductory computer literacy classes, typing, file management, using the internet, and essential software tools, before progressing to coding fundamentals and career-track specialisation.

Curriculum
📅

December 2026 Launch

The pilot is timed to coincide with Kenya's high school graduation season, giving school leavers a direct path into tech education at exactly the moment they need it before opportunities pass them by.

Path to a Tech Career

From basics to job-ready: students progress through computer literacy, introductory coding, and then choose a specialist track; Software Engineering, Data Science, Cybersecurity, AI/ML, Cloud Engineering, and more supported throughout by industry mentors.
